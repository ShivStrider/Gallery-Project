# Google Play Store Release Package

## 1) App Listing Copy

### App title / subtitle
- **App title (max 30 chars):** Face Album
- **Subtitle / short tagline (internal reference):** Find similar faces fast

### Short description (max 80 chars)
- **Primary short description:** Find photos with similar faces from your gallery in minutes.

### Long description (Play listing)
- **Draft long description:**

Face Album helps you organize large photo libraries by finding visually similar faces directly on your device. Pick a reference photo, scan your gallery, and review grouped matches quickly.

Why people use Face Album:
- Fast similarity scan for large local galleries
- Side-by-side review experience for easy decisions
- Export selected matches for follow-up organization
- On-device processing flow designed for privacy-first usage

Suggested use cases:
- Find all photos of one person across years of memories
- Prepare albums for family events and reunions
- Speed up manual cleanup of duplicate or near-duplicate portraits

Face Album is built for users who want practical face-similarity search without complicated setup.

## 6) Rollback Criteria (Defined)

Rollback is triggered if any condition is met after release:

1. **Crash threshold breached:**
   - Crash-free users drops below **99.5%** in any 24-hour window.
2. **ANR threshold breached:**
   - ANR rate exceeds **0.47%** overall OR **0.24%** user-perceived in Play vitals.
3. **Top user complaints trigger:**
   - 10+ materially similar complaints in 24 hours about:
     - app not scanning,
     - missing gallery photos,
     - severe battery/thermal impact,
     - data-loss perception.

### Rollback actions
- Pause staged rollout immediately.
- Triage incident within 2 hours.
- Decide hotfix vs full rollback within 4 hours.
- Publish status update in release channel.
